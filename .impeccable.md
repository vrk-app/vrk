## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are customer coordinators, contractor operators, dispatchers, and adjacent B2B service staff working inside request-driven workflows.
- They use the product to route work, inspect status, verify evidence, coordinate execution, and close operational loops without ambiguity.
- They work in dense, process-heavy environments where trust, speed of comprehension, and traceability matter more than novelty.

### Brand Personality
- Industrial
- Utilitarian
- High-clarity
- Calm under operational pressure
- Trustworthy and audit-friendly

### Aesthetic Direction
- Desktop-first operator surfaces with solid mobile behavior.
- Dense but composed layouts: strong hierarchy, visible state, deliberate spacing, and minimal decorative noise.
- Interfaces should feel like professional operational tooling, not consumer SaaS or marketing landing pages.
- Use restrained motion only where it clarifies transitions or status changes.
- Explicitly avoid glassmorphism, playful consumer tropes, nested card grids, glow-heavy gradients, decorative clutter, and generic AI-admin aesthetics.

### Design Principles
- Make state obvious: status, ownership, evidence, and next action must be scannable within seconds.
- Prefer legibility over spectacle: information hierarchy, contrast, and operational clarity beat visual novelty.
- Keep density calm: compact layouts are welcome, but only with clear grouping and rhythm.
- Design for trust: every important action should feel deliberate, reviewable, and reversible where appropriate.
- Build for work, not demos: empty states, errors, loading states, and edge cases are first-class UI surfaces.
